You asked how big this market is expected to get. Short answer, it's growing fast and it just got a real boost. The ADHD treatment market overall is around $14.5B now and headed to $18-20B by 2030. The thing I'd really flag is Medicare. Starting in 2025 Medicare began paying for digital mental health apps, and as of this past January that coverage now includes ADHD specifically, which is exactly our space. They also said it's not limited to those conditions, so it can expand. That's a big deal for us, because it turns this from a "someday" market into one where doctors can actually get paid to prescribe this kind of product now.
