The thing I'd really flag is Medicare. Starting in 2025 Medicare began paying for digital mental health apps, and as of this past January that coverage now includes ADHD specifically, which is exactly our space. They also said it's not limited to those conditions, so it can expand. That's a big deal for us, because it turns this from a "someday" market into one where doctors can actually get paid to prescribe this kind of product now.
