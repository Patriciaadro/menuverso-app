The thing I'd really flag for Candice is Medicare. Starting in 2025, CMS began paying for digital mental health treatments, and as of this past January, under CMS's 2026 physician fee schedule, that coverage now includes ADHD specifically, which is exactly our space (the new billing code is G0552). That's a big deal, because it turns this from a "someday" market into one where doctors can actually get paid to prescribe this kind of product right now.
